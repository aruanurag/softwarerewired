AI-native products need a different backbone: reliable data flows, strong observability, and guardrails that protect user trust.

Key patterns worth adopting early:

- Event pipelines that capture human feedback.
- Policy layers for model access, prompt control, and escalation paths.
- Evaluation suites that evolve as product capabilities expand.

Architecture that treats AI as a first-class dependency will move faster and break less.
