Agentic systems blur the line between software and operations. Instead of static menus, users now expect outcomes: draft the proposal, resolve the ticket, close the deal.

Three shifts define the new stack:

1. Value moves from features to outcomes.
2. Interfaces become conversational and adaptive.
3. Pricing aligns with work completed, not seats sold.

The next wave of SaaS will be judged by the quality of its agents and the reliability of their execution.
